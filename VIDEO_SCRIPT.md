# Proof of Sweat — demo video script (~90 seconds)

Simple spoken English. Read the **Say** lines aloud; follow the **Do** lines on screen.

---

### 1. The hook (0:00 - 0:12)
**Do:** Show the home page (hero with the three result cards).
**Say:**
"Hiring someone online is easy. Knowing they did real work, and did not just copy from ChatGPT, is hard. Proof of Sweat fixes that. An AI jury checks the work, and the money is paid only if the work is real."

### 2. Show real results (0:12 - 0:32)
**Do:** Scroll the list of tasks. Click the green "Genuine work" task.
**Say:**
"Here are tasks that were already checked. For each one, a group of AIs read the delivered work and gave a verdict, in plain words. This one is real work, so the worker got paid. The AI even explains why."

**Do:** Close it, click the red "Plagiarized" task.
**Say:**
"This one was copied from a website. The AI caught it and blocked the payment."

### 3. Try it live (0:32 - 1:12)
**Do:** Click "Connect MetaMask".
**Say:** "Now let me try it myself. First I connect my wallet."

**Do:** Open a task marked "Open", click "Claim & stake".
**Say:** "I pick an open task and claim it. I put down a small stake, so I have skin in the game."

**Do:** Click "Submit for review", paste the work link.
**Say:** "I submit my work as a simple link."

**Do:** Click "Run the AI jury". (Fast-forward the wait in editing.)
**Say:** "Then I ask the AI jury to judge it. A few different AIs read my work and vote."

**Do:** Verdict shows "Genuine work".
**Say:** "They agree it is real work."

**Do:** Click "Withdraw".
**Say:** "So I withdraw my reward. Real money, paid for real work."

### 4. Why GenLayer (1:12 - 1:28)
**Do:** Scroll to the "Why GenLayer" section (the comparison).
**Say:**
"A normal blockchain cannot do this. It cannot read a web page, and it cannot judge if work is real or fake. GenLayer can, because it has AI built in, and many AIs must agree. Take that away, and this app cannot exist."

### 5. Close (1:28 - 1:32)
**Do:** Back to the home page.
**Say:** "Proof of Sweat. Get paid for real work. AI slop gets nothing."

---

**Tips**
- The "Run the AI jury" step takes about 30 to 90 seconds. Record it, then speed that part up in editing.
- If the jury says "undetermined", just click "Run the AI jury" again.
- Reading the tasks and verdicts needs no wallet, so you can film section 2 without connecting.
