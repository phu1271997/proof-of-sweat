> Follow the **[Do]** actions on your screen and read the **[Say]** lines aloud.

"In the freelance and gig economy, hiring someone online is easy. But knowing if they did real work, or just copy-pasted from ChatGPT, is almost impossible.  
This is **Proof of Sweat** — a decentralized bounty platform on GenLayer. Here, workers get paid only when an on-chain AI jury agrees the work is genuine."

### 2. Connect Wallet & Multichain (0:18 – 0:35)
**[Do]** Click **"Launch app"** to go to `/app`. Click **"Connect Wallet"** in the top right to open the wallet modal. Highlight the supported wallets: MetaMask, OKX Wallet, and Rabby. Connect your wallet. Then point to the chain switcher: **GenLayer** and **Arc**.  
**[Say]**  
"Let's enter the app.  
We support popular Web3 wallets including MetaMask, OKX Wallet, and Rabby.  
Proof of Sweat is also multichain: GenLayer handles the AI jury judgment, while Arc provides fast settlement in native USDC."

### 3. Client Flow: Post a Bounty (0:35 – 0:55)
**[Do]** Click the **"Post"** tab in the navigation bar. Click the blue **"use sample"** button in the top right of the form to auto-fill the task details. Show the Title, Spec, and Rules. Click **"Escrow reward & post"** and confirm the transaction in your wallet.  
**[Say]**  
"As a client, I can post a bounty.  
I'll click 'use sample' to quickly fill in our specification and rules — requiring original developer research with no AI slop.  
I escrow 2 GEN as the reward, set a 1 GEN worker stake, and post it to the blockchain."

Once claimed, paste the deliverable URL into the box and click **"Submit for review"**.

## 🎬 Tips for Recording

1. **Prepare 2 Wallets beforehand**:
   - Wallet A (Client): Has some GEN.
   - Wallet B (Worker): Has some GEN.
2. **Speed up the wait in editing**:
   - The AI Jury takes about 30 to 45 seconds on-chain. Cut or fast-forward that part so the video stays punchy.
3. **Spec-Match Highlight**:
   - If judges ask about the AI intelligence, point out that the jury checks both **Confidence** (is it human?) AND **Spec-Match** (is it on-topic?), protecting clients from fake or off-topic submissions.
4. **Resolution**:
   - Record at 1080p or 4K with clean browser zoom (110% or 125% zoom makes fonts and buttons very crisp on video).
